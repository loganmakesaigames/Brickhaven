# Brickhaven

An open world built from toy bricks: villages to visit, people to talk to, animals to ride, things to build and things to blow up. The clouds, the sun and the moon hang on strings like props on a film set.

**Play it: https://loganmakesaigames.github.io/brickhaven/**

It runs in the browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer. Nothing to install.

## On a phone

Open the link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and turn the phone sideways. To keep it like an app, add it to your home screen: in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full screen with its own icon, and still works without a connection once it has been opened.

- **Left thumb**: move (push past the ring to run)
- **Right thumb**: drag to look, tap to build, hold on a piece to take it apart
- **Two fingers**: zoom the camera
- **Buttons**: jump (bottom right); map, journal, view and graphics (top right)
- Prompts like "Talk to …" or "Ride the horse" are buttons: just tap them

If it stutters, tap the sparkle button (top right) to switch the graphics to **Fast**.

## On a computer

`W A S D` to move, `Shift` to run, `Space` to jump, the mouse to look (click the game first), left mouse to take pieces apart, right mouse to build, `R` to turn a piece, `1`–`9` for the hotbar, `E` to talk, ride, sit or open, `M` for the map and `J` for the journal.
